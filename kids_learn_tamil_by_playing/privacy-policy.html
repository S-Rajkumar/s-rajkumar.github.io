Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This privacy policy describes how Kids: Learn Tamil by Playing ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when a child or a parent uses it. The app is published by UDMNXPDU and its Android package name is com.udmnxpdu.kids_learn_tamil_by_playing.

The app was built child-safety-first, and the short version is this: it collects nothing. There is no login, no account, no chat, no social feature, and no text field anywhere in the app that a child could type into. Everything a child earns stays on their own device.

Information we collect

  • The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.
  • There is no account, no email address, no phone number and no sign-in of any kind.
  • The app never asks for a child's name, age, photograph, location or contacts, and contains no text input field in which any of those could be entered.
  • No analytics, advertising-attribution or crash-reporting SDK is included in the app.
  • The app requests no runtime permissions — no camera, no microphone, no location, no contacts and no storage access.

What is stored, and where

The app saves a child's progress — coins, experience, which Tamil letters have been written and how well, which words have been answered, what is planted in the garden, which character and pet are equipped, and the music and sound switches — using standard local app storage on the device itself. None of it is synced to a server or a cloud service, and none of it identifies anybody. Uninstalling the app removes all of it permanently.

Advertising

The app contains one advertisement: an optional rewarded video from Google AdMob, which lives inside the Parent Zone and can only be reached by first passing a parental gate. It is never shown automatically, never interrupts play, and never appears anywhere a child can reach on their own. There are no banner ads, no interstitials and no ads of any kind on any game or home screen.

Because the app is directed at children, every ad request it can make is configured as child-directed:

  • tagged for child-directed treatment (COPPA);
  • tagged as being for a user under the age of consent (GDPR);
  • limited to a maximum ad content rating of G; and
  • marked non-personalized, so the device's advertising identifier is not used to build or apply an advertising profile.

Google AdMob still receives the technical information it needs to return an ad, such as the device type, coarse country and the app making the request. See Google's privacy policy for how AdMob handles ad requests.

In-app purchases

There are none. Every character, pet and scene in the shop is earned by playing — with coins, levels or Tamil letters written — and no part of the app can spend real money.

Speech

The app pronounces Tamil words aloud using the text-to-speech engine already installed on the device. Only the Tamil word being taught is passed to it. Nothing about the child is sent, and the app has no microphone permission and cannot listen.

Sharing a picture

The Parent Zone can create a picture card of the child's character and scene, and hand it to the device's own share sheet so a grown-up can send it to a family member if they choose. The image is written to the app's private cache folder, needs no storage permission, is never uploaded anywhere by the app, and is only ever sent if an adult picks a destination themselves.

Network use

The only network request the app makes is the rewarded ad described above. If a grown-up never opens it, the app never contacts the internet at all, and every game, letter and word works fully offline.

Children's privacy

This app is directed at children and was designed around that from the start. It complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and with Google Play's Families policy. It has no login, no accounts, no chat, no user-generated content, no social features and no outbound link a child can reach. Advertising, links out of the app and the button that closes it all sit behind the same parental gate, which asks a simple sum and replaces the whole sum on a wrong answer so that it cannot be passed by elimination.

Because the app collects no personal information from anybody, it collects none from children either, and there is no parental-consent flow to complete for data collection that does not happen.

Your choices

Since no account exists and nothing leaves the device, there is nothing held by us to access, correct or delete. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored progress from the device. A parent who would prefer their child never see the rewarded ad simply need not open it — nothing in the app prompts for it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
