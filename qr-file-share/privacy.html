Privacy Policy

QR File Share — Last updated: August 5, 2026

Overview

QR File Share ("the app") is a peer-to-peer file transfer tool that lets you send files, folders, and text between devices connected to the same local Wi-Fi network or mobile hotspot. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and what is — and is not — collected.

QR File Share does not use the internet or any cloud server to transfer your files. All file and text transfers happen directly between your devices over your local network. We do not have access to, store, or see the content you share.

Permissions the app requests

Permission Why it's needed
Camera To scan the QR code shown on the sending device when using Receive.
Storage / Files access To let you choose files or folders to send, and to save files you download to your chosen location.
Wi-Fi / Network state To detect your device's local IP address so a QR code can be generated for nearby devices to connect to.
Internet (local network only) Used only to run a temporary local server on your device during a Send session and to connect to that server during a Receive session. No data leaves your local network.

What data we collect

QR File Share does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data, file contents, or usage analytics on external servers. Files you choose to share are transferred directly between your device and the receiving device over your local network only, for the duration of the active session.

Clipboard access

When you use the text-sharing feature, the app may read text you have copied to generate a QR code, and may write scanned text back to your clipboard on the receiving device. This clipboard data is never sent anywhere outside your device and is not stored by the app.

Advertising

This app may display banner advertisements served through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads, in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can review or adjust ad personalization settings in your device's Google Ads settings.

Data sharing with third parties

Other than the advertising SDK mentioned above, QR File Share does not share any data with third parties. There are no analytics trackers, no account system, and no remote database associated with this app.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated occasionally to reflect changes in the app's features or legal requirements. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please reach out via the contact details listed on the developer's portfolio page.
