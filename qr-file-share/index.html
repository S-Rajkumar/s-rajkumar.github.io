QR File Share

Share files and folders instantly between devices on the same network. No internet, no cables, no cloud. Just scan and transfer.

In development Android Windows

Overview

QR File Share lets you send files and whole folders straight from one device to another, as long as they're on the same network. There's no account to create, no file to upload anywhere first, and no internet connection required — one device shows a QR code, the other scans it, and the transfer starts.
