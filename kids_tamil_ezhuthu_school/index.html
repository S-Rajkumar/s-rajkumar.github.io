Teaches children all 247 Tamil characters through guided handwriting tracing, built on real stroke-order data — not a guess derived from a font.

In development Android

Overview

A Tamil-first Flutter app for children aged roughly 4–8, teaching every one of Tamil's 247 characters — the 12 vowels, 18 consonants, and all 216 vowel-consonant combinations — plus 6 additional characters used for borrowed sounds. Each letter is traced on a landscape canvas across four difficulty levels, from a fully guided tube-and-waypoint mode down to a freehand shape exam, with every layer of the canvas — the guide, the fill, the scoring — derived from the same stroke path so nothing on screen ever disagrees with what's being measured.

Stroke order couldn't be guessed from a font: a typeface has the correct letterform but no notion of what a hand draws first, so each character's writing order comes from a dedicated authoring pipeline and a stroke-order reference, verified by hand rather than shipped unchecked. Beyond tracing, a Word Builder game teaches children to compose real Tamil words by dragging and combining the 31 base characters — nothing pre-built is ever handed over. The whole app is built child-safety-first: no login, no accounts, no chat, no analytics, and every byte of a child's progress and drawings stays on the device.
