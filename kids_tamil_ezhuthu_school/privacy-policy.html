Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

This privacy policy describes how Kids: அஆஇஈ தமிழ் எழுத்து — 247 ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when a child or parent uses it. The app was built child-safety-first: there is no login, no account, no chat, and no social sharing anywhere a child can reach.

Information we collect

  • This app does not collect or transmit any personal data.
  • A child's tracing progress, stars, coins and drawings are stored only in local storage on the device and never leave it.
  • There is no account, no email address, and no sign-in of any kind.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK is included in the app.

How we use information

  • Progress is used only to show the same child their own stars, coins and completed letters the next time they open the app.
  • We do not sell, share or transmit any information to third parties, because none is collected in the first place.

Data storage & security

All progress is stored locally on the device using standard local app storage. Nothing is synced to a server or cloud service. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored progress.

Third-party services

The app's Parent Zone — reachable only behind a parental gate — includes optional rewarded video ads from Google AdMob, used to let a grown-up earn in-app coins. These requests are marked non-personalized and are tagged as child-directed, so the advertising identifier is not used for ad personalization. No advertising appears anywhere else in the app, and there are no banner ads. See Google's privacy policy for how AdMob itself handles ad requests.

Your choices

Since no account exists, there is nothing to delete on our end. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored progress from the device.

Children's privacy

This app is directed at children and was designed around that from the start: no login, no accounts, no chat, no social features, and no outbound link a child can reach without a parent first passing a parental gate. Purchases, ad settings and any outbound link all sit behind that same gate.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to udmnxpdu.support@gmail.com.
